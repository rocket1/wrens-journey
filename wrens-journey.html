Wren's Journey

A Skeptic's Nightmare

An albino orphan, a borrowed father's voice on a dying cassette tape, and a wall no one has opened in five hundred years. Wren's Journey is a short Infocom-style text adventure — compiled to real Z-machine bytecode and running live in your browser.
